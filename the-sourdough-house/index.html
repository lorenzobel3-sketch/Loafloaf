Questions people actually ask.

Why does it cost more than the one in the supermarket?

Because of the thirty-six hours. Three of the four ingredients cost pennies. You are paying for the time nobody else will give the bread, plus flour that is worth fermenting in the first place.

How do I tell real sourdough from the other kind?

Read the ingredients. Four is right. If you see yeast, oil, sugar, vinegar or enzymes, it was raised fast and given a sourdough label. The other tell is the price of speed, a loaf that goes stale hard by Tuesday.

Why only Sundays?

The bake runs Saturday night and the loaves come out in the early hours. One bake, one batch, one morning. Baking twice would mean cutting the rest short, and the rest is the point.

Is it gluten free?

No. It is made of wheat flour and it contains gluten. The long ferment changes the flavour and the texture, and we are not going to tell you it does anything for your health.

How long does it keep?

Cut side down on a board, four or five days. It will not go stale the way a fast loaf does. Day three toasted is the best it gets.
